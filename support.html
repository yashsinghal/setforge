Support

Questions, bugs or feature ideas: we read everything.

Contact us: open a request at github.com/yashsinghal/setforge/issues. Please don't include health details or anything private in a public request.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Setforge works fully offline on one device. Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code under Profile › Account to sync across devices and chat with Spot's AI.

The Watch isn't counting my reps.
Wear the watch on the hand that moves with the weight, keep it snug, and check that Motion & Fitness is allowed for Setforge in the Watch app. You can always tap the screen to count reps by hand.

How do I connect Apple Health?
Open the Health app › Profile › Apps › Setforge and turn on the data types you want to share.

How do I stop Spot sharing data with the AI?
In Spot, tap ••• › Settings and turn off "Share workout data with AI". The offline coach keeps working.

How do I delete my account?
Profile › Account › Delete account. This removes your account and all cloud data immediately. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app.
